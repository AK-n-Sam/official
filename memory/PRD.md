# NexusOS — SME Business Management Platform (PRD)

## Original Problem Statement
Build a production-structured, fully runnable foundation for an all-in-one Business Management Platform for SMEs, so future prompts can add modules without restructuring. Must include real working auth, app shell, core data models with multi-tenant isolation, a reusable CRUD engine, a live dashboard, design system, seed data, and settings.

## Architecture
- **Frontend**: React 19 (CRA/craco), TailwindCSS + shadcn/ui, react-router-dom v7, recharts, sonner, lucide-react.
- **Backend**: FastAPI + Motor (async MongoDB). All routes under `/api`.
- **DB**: MongoDB. UUID string `id` per document (no ObjectId exposure). Every record scoped by `org_id`.
- **Auth**: Dual — JWT email/password + Emergent-managed Google login. Bearer token in localStorage (`bmp_token`) + httpOnly cookies. `get_current_user` resolves JWT or session token, returns org-scoped user.
- **Multi-tenancy**: user has `org_ids[]` + `active_org_id`; workspace switch updates `active_org_id`; all queries filter by it.

## User Personas
- SME owner/operator managing sales, invoicing, inventory, customers, team and tasks in one workspace.
- Users may belong to multiple businesses (workspaces) and switch between them.

## Core Requirements (static)
Auth & workspaces · App shell (sidebar, header, workspace switcher, notifications, global search placeholder, user menu, responsive) · 12 module routes · Core data models with relationships + org ownership · Reusable CRUD engine · Live dashboard from real data · Design system (dark default + light toggle) · Multi-currency (USD/EUR/GBP/INR) · Realistic seed data · Settings.

## Iteration 10 — Differentiation & Simplicity: "record what happened, the paperwork follows" (2026-10-01)
- **Core principle**: the user records business events (a sale, money received, stock arriving); NexusOS updates every connected record. The product is organised around what the owner is doing, not around database modules.
- **One-step business actions** (`components/actions/*`, available from anywhere via `useActions()`):
  - **Make a sale** (`POST /api/actions/sale`): type-to-find customer, or type a new name (created, or reused if the name/email already exists); pick products by search (price + stock shown, same product twice = qty 2) or type a custom item; Paid now / Pay later / Part paid. Creates the invoice, deducts stock, records the payment. Due date, tax, notes, line discounts and "save as draft" sit behind *More options*. Also replaces the "New invoice" form (starts on Pay later). Remembers the last payment choice and method.
  - **Get paid** (`POST /api/customers/{id}/payments`): one amount, spread over the customer's unpaid invoices oldest-due first, with a preview of exactly which invoices it pays off.
  - **Buy stock** (`POST /api/actions/purchase`): products, quantities, unit cost (last cost prefilled), supplier (prefilled from the product), paid or not → stock in + one "Stock purchases" expense; updates the product's purchase price. Replaces "stock movement + separately typed expense". The old movement dialog is now **Adjust stock** (count / removed / added without cost).
  - **Follow up**: a task for yourself, linked to the customer, due Tomorrow / In 3 days / Next week in one tap.
  - **Send reminder** (`GET /api/invoices/{id}/reminder`, `POST …/remind`): a payment-chasing email written for how late the invoice is (amount, number, due date, org name), opened in the user's mail app or copied; logged on the invoice (`reminders[]`) and shown on the invoice and the customer's timeline.
- **Today** (`/today`, `GET /api/today`, new home): one queue of concrete things needing the user, across modules, each resolvable in place: overdue invoices (Record payment / Send reminder), due within 3 days, drafts (Mark sent), low/out of stock (Restock → Buy stock prefilled), unpaid bills (Mark paid), my tasks due/late (Done / Tomorrow), won-but-unconverted deals (Make customer → offer a sale), and **regular customers who have gone quiet** (≥3 orders, gap > max(45 days, 2× their usual interval) → Follow up). One-click resolutions show **Undo** instead of confirmation dialogs; any item can be snoozed (`snoozes` collection per user; `POST/DELETE /api/today/snooze`); sending a reminder snoozes that invoice for 3 days. Summary: money in this week, sold this week, owed to you (overdue part). "Recently" activity feed below.
- **Customer = one place**: header actions Make a sale / Get paid / Follow up / Edit; per-invoice Remind; an **Activity** timeline merging sales, payments, reminders, tasks, lead origin and **notes** (`customer_notes`, `POST/DELETE /api/customers/{id}/notes`).
- **Navigation 15 → 7**: Today · Customers (Customers | Pipeline) · Invoices (Invoices | Payments) · Spending (Expenses | Suppliers) · Products (Products | Stock) · Team (Tasks | People) · Insights (Overview | Reports), plus Settings and Help. Related pages are tabs (`SectionSwitch`). Header "Create" menu → **Make a sale** button + action menu; the command palette lists actions first ("Do") and understands words like "restock", "paid", "sell".
- **Removed / consolidated**: My Work page (→ Today; `/my-work` redirects), Dashboard's tabs, action list, at-a-glance counters, recent lists and activity (Overview is analysis only; Today is action), the invoice summary cards (merged into the status views, which now show count + money), separate Sent / Partially paid tabs (→ Unpaid; old links map), table/card view toggle (chosen by screen width), the duplicate "Compact rows" item in the account menu, `AiInsightsPlaceholder`/`ActionCenter` components. Renamed: Sales → **Payments** (`/sales` redirects), Inventory → **Stock**, Employees → **People**, Leads → **Pipeline**, Dashboard → **Overview**.

## Iteration 11 — Bank feeds (2026-10-01)
- **Bank page** (`/bank`, owners/admins only; `backend/bank.py`, router `/api/bank/*`): money in and out of the bank, each line matched to the books and confirmed in one click.
- **Two ways in**:
  - **Statement import** (free, any bank, any country): CSV, OFX or QFX downloaded from online banking. The header row is found below any preamble; date/description/amount (or money in + money out, or a DR/CR column) are detected for US, UK, EU and Indian formats; day-first vs month-first dates are resolved from the data, falling back to the workspace currency (USD → month-first); European amounts (1.234,56), parentheses and DR/CR suffixes parse. Preview before import; columns and date format can be corrected. Re-importing overlapping statements skips lines already imported (OFX FITID, or a hash of date+amount+description+occurrence so two identical same-day lines both stay).
  - **Plaid** (live): Link → `public_token` exchange → accounts (depository and credit only) → `/transactions/sync` with a stored cursor (pending lines skipped; Plaid's sign flipped so money in is positive; bank-side removals honoured) → balances. Access tokens are encrypted at rest (Fernet, key derived from `JWT_SECRET`) and never sent to the browser. Removing the last account of a connection calls `/item/remove`. Auto-sync when the page opens (hourly at most) plus a Sync button. Switched on by `PLAID_CLIENT_ID` / `PLAID_SECRET` / `PLAID_ENV` (see `backend/.env.example`; `PLAID_BASE_URL` overrides the host for testing). Plaid's Trial plan (US/Canada teams created on or after 2026-04-15) is free for up to 10 connected banks with real data; Sandbox is free with test banks.
- **Suggestions** (never applied without confirmation): money in → unpaid invoice by number (+60), exact balance (+40), customer name (+30), part payments allowed, never overpaying; money out → unpaid bill with the same amount (marks it paid), an expense already entered within ±5 days (links it, nothing counted twice), a known supplier/vendor with its usual category, or a new expense needing a category; transfers ("transfer", "tfr", "savings", "own account"…) → Ignore. Green / amber / grey confidence; **Confirm N sure matches** applies only the green ones. Every confirmation is undoable (removes the payment / expense or restores the bill's status); removing a bank-created payment from the invoice page puts the bank line back to review. Today shows "Confirm N bank transactions" for owners/admins.
- **Verified**: `tests/test_bank_parsing.py` (8: US, UK with preamble, Indian, European/DR-CR, amount formats, OFX, suggestions incl. transfers, token encryption), `tests/test_bank_api.py` (7, including the full Plaid flow against a local stand-in that mimics Plaid's request/response shapes; the Plaid test skips without `MOCK_PLAID_URL`); all suites **100/100**; 14 browser checks for the Bank page (import → preview → confirm → undo → expense → ignore → Today → bulk confirm → member view → mobile), plus the 23 + 30 earlier browser checks. **Not verified**: Plaid's real hosted Link window and real banks (no Plaid keys available here).

## Iteration 12 — Automation & API orchestration (2026-10-01)
- **AI key pool** (`backend/integrations/`): every AI call goes through `generate_text()` → provider order (default Gemini → OpenRouter → OpenAI, changeable) → healthiest, least-recently-used key → model list per provider → call → classify → retry / rest / switch → normalized result + telemetry (`api_calls`, 30-day TTL). Keys come only from `OPENAI_API_KEYS` / `GEMINI_API_KEYS` / `OPENROUTER_API_KEYS` (comma-separated pools) in the git-ignored `.env`; state in `api_key_state` holds a sha256 fingerprint and a mask (`sk-p…xRwA`), never the key; errors are redacted before they are stored.
  - Failure classes: **rate limited** (rest for Retry-After / Gemini retryDelay, else 30s→60s→2m… capped at 15m), **quota exhausted** (parked until the next UTC reset), **auth error** (quarantined until a health check or an administrator puts it back), **transient** (one quick retry on the same key; repeated blips rest it), **model unavailable** (retired/restricted/upstream-busy model or empty answer → same key, next model; the key is not blamed; the working model is remembered), **bad request** (skip the provider). Generation is read-only, so retries can't duplicate business operations. Identical requests can be served from `ai_cache`.
  - Verified live (2026-10-01): Gemini key works (`gemini-3.5-flash-lite`; `gemini-2.5-*` are closed to new users), OpenRouter key works on free models (`openrouter/free`, nemotron-3-ultra:free), OpenAI key is valid but the account has **no credits** (classified quota exhausted, parked). Two classifier traps found against real responses and fixed: OpenRouter answers **403** for app-restricted models (must not quarantine the key) and Gemini words **per-minute** limits like a quota error (must not park the key until midnight).
- **Automation engine** (`backend/automation/`): `emit()` records business events (`events`, 180-day TTL) and runs rules (Trigger → Condition → Action → Result → log in `automation_log`); a persistent job queue (`jobs`: atomic claim with lease, backoff retries, `Defer` for "try later", dead jobs → needs attention + Retry); a scheduler that turns schedules into jobs keyed by time slot (`sched:<name>:<slot>` unique), so restarts or extra workers can't double-run a slot; per-effect idempotency keys (`automation_effects`). Runs inside the API process (`AUTOMATION_DISABLED=1` turns it off per process).
  - Events: customer_created, invoice_created, invoice_overdue (exactly once per transition: overdue flips are per-invoice and guarded), payment_received, payment_removed, invoice_cancelled, invoice_deleted, inventory_low (once per crossing of the reorder level), stock_purchased, lead_converted, bank_imported.
  - Rules: close the collection follow-up when an invoice is paid / cancelled / deleted; low-stock note; new bank lines → categorise job.
  - Schedules: overdue check (15 min, all workspaces, even with nobody signed in), repeating invoices/bills (hourly), collection follow-ups (daily: invoices unpaid N days after due, default 14, get one task for the seller; shown on Today merged with the invoice, not twice), clean-up (daily), AI health check (30 min, free model-list calls; recovered keys return), bank sync (6 h, if Plaid is set up), weekly summary (per workspace, ISO week).
- **Repeating invoices and bills** (`recurring`): **Repeat** on an invoice (owners/admins) or an expense row. Invoices are either prepared for **approval** (default; Today shows Approve / Skip) or issued automatically; bills are added as unpaid. Monthly schedules keep their day (31st → 28th → 31st). One invoice per (schedule, period) guaranteed twice over: an effect key and an existence check on `recurring_id + recurring_period`; approval decisions are claimed atomically so a double click can't issue twice. A deleted customer pauses the schedule with a reason.
- **Approvals** (`approvals`): generic prepare → approve → execute with registered executors; failed executions stay retryable; decisions are logged.
- **Bank categories**: keyword rules for common merchants first (free), then AI in batches of 40 for the rest (only descriptions are sent, long digit runs stripped); suggestions stay medium confidence, so bulk confirm never creates AI-guessed expenses; if no AI provider is available the job defers and retries.
- **Weekly summary**: figures computed locally → AI writes headline / 2-3 sentences / next steps from them (told not to invent numbers) → falls back to a standard template; a quiet week skips AI. Shown on Overview for owners/admins with **Update** (runs as a background job: Queued → Writing → Done).
- **Admin views**: Settings > **Automation** (switches, approvals, repeating items, "What happened" log, background work with Retry) and Settings > **Integrations** (AI / Bank / Email status words; the platform administrator, i.e. `ADMIN_EMAIL`, also sees the masked key pool with per-key health, success rate, latency, cooldowns, 24 h usage and failovers, can reorder/disable providers, check now and put keys back in use). Today shows "Done for you" and approvals / failed jobs for owners/admins.
- **Verified**: `tests/test_ai_failover.py` (14: scenarios A–G plus model fallback, back-off growth, cache, status words and real provider error messages), `tests/test_automation_units.py` (6: schedule maths, slots, keyword categories, redaction, and a scan proving no configured key is in any source file); ESLint clean; bundle builds. `tests/test_automation_api.py` (13, end to end through the API) is written; see the iteration report for its run status.
